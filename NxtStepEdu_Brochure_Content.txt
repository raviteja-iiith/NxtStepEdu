NxtStepEdu ERP

[COMPANY INTRODUCTION]

- Multi-Tenant Cloud Architecture: 100% data privacy and cross-school data isolation.
- Zero-Paperwork Operations: Complete digital workflow for admissions, fees, timetables, and communication.
- 1-Click Bulk Onboarding: Fast Excel template import for thousands of students, parents, and teachers.
- Unified Communication Engine: In-app notices, broadcast announcements, urgent priority badges, and SMS alerts.

[TITLE & TAGLINE]
Teacher Portal
Focus on Teaching — Let the System Handle the Paperwork

[CORE FOCUS]
Minimizing administrative overhead for educators while equipping them with easy-to-use tools for daily attendance, grading, homework, and student tracking.

[KEY FEATURES & IMPORTANT POINTS]
- 📋 2-Minute Daily Attendance: Class teachers mark section attendance (Present, Absent, Late, Excused) in under 2 minutes using a 1-click "Mark All Present" shortcut.
- 📝 Direct Exam Marks Entry: Fast entry of student exam scores per subject/section; results instantly update the Principal dashboard and Parent cards without duplicate data entry.
- 📚 Homework & Assignment Hub: Create and manage assignments, set submission deadlines, upload digital study materials, and track student submission statuses.
- 📖 Lesson Plans & Resource Library: Organize digital lesson plans by chapter/date and maintain a centralized repository of teaching materials for every class.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 My Students & Parent Directory: Access detailed student profiles, academic history, attendance records, and linked parent emergency contacts in one place.
- 🔑 On-The-Spot Parent PIN Resets: Resolve parent forgotten PIN requests directly from the teacher portal without burdening school administration.
- 🏖️ Digital Leave Requests: Submit leave applications with date ranges and reasons; track approval status in real time.
- 🏫 Smart Role Switching: Tailored portal experience that distinguishes between Class Teacher responsibilities (attendance, parent management) and Subject Teacher duties (grading, assignments).
